Please ensure that:

- ✅ Your files open correctly
- ✅ Your calculations are accurate
- ✅ Your charts and visualizations are readable
- ✅ You uploaded the correct file
- ✅ You followed the instructions

And most importantly:

```text
Did you validate your data source?
```

Because fixing a typo is easy.

## 📊 Business Analytics Wisdom

In Business Analytics, numbers are important.

But more important than the numbers are the questions behind them.

You will often encounter situations where:

- The data is incomplete
- The trends look suspicious
- The report says one thing
- The stakeholders believe another
- And the spreadsheet somehow contains 17 worksheets named "Sheet1"

Do not panic.

This is normal.

Probably.

---

## 🚀 Analytics Mindset

A good analyst does not simply create reports.

A good analyst asks:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What is likely to happen next?
- What should we do about it?

The goal is not merely to produce charts.

The goal is to transform information into decisions.
